Privacy Policy — Suimei 八字命書 - 生辰命盤分析

Effective date: August 2026

1. Data Collection

Suimei 八字命書 is a fully offline, local-only application. All chart data you enter (name, birth date/time, gender, birthplace) is stored exclusively in a local database on your own device. It is never uploaded to any server or third party, and the developer has no ability to access, view, or retrieve this data.

The app does not require an account, does not collect analytics, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party tracking SDKs of any kind.

2. Payment Processing

If you choose to purchase the optional PDF export unlock, payment is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, according to their own privacy policies. The developer does not directly collect, receive, or store any payment information; purchase records are held by the platform only.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Fonts to load font resources for on-screen display and for generating PDF exports. This may result in a network request to Google's servers to download font files. No personal or chart data is included in these requests. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on this service.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored only on your device, you are always in full control of it. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data. There is no server-side copy to separately request deletion of.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone in that age group — consistent with the app collecting no personal information from any user, of any age.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.
